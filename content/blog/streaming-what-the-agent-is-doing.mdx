A spinner tells the user that something is happening. For an agent, that is
almost useless: the interesting question is not *whether* it is working but
*what it just decided to do*.

Things I want to answer here:

- Why a request/response API is the wrong shape for an agent, and what a
  persistent connection changes.
- What a tool call looks like on the wire, and how much of it belongs on screen.
- Where to draw the line between "useful transparency" and "firehose".
